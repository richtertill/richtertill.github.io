### Graduate Level

- **Statistical Learning** (SS24)

  - Master's course focusing on fundamental concepts and modern applications of statistical learning methods

  - Organizer since WS24
  - Advanced seminar covering current research topics in deep learning

### Undergraduate Level

- **Analysis for Informatics** (WS23-WS24)
  - Bachelor's course covering mathematical foundations for computer science

- Mentoring of internship students
