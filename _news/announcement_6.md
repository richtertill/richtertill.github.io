:globe_with_meridians: The **Learning Meaningful Representations of Life** (LMRL) Workshop I co-organized at ICLR 2025 in Singapore was a great success! The workshop brought together researchers working on foundation models for biological data, multimodal representation learning, and multiscale biological representations, featuring exciting keynotes from leaders in the field and stimulating discussions about the future of AI in biology.
